Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

What Editor Lautaro is

Editor Lautaro is a personal desktop application that runs on the creator's own computer. It edits short videos and, when the creator asks, uploads them to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts.

Information we access

  • TikTok basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar), through Login with TikTok, only to show which account is connected.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok, used only to upload videos the creator chooses to send.
  • Videos the creator edits and selects to send to TikTok.

How information is stored

All data stays on the creator's own computer. Editor Lautaro has no servers, does not keep a database of users, and does not sell, share or transfer any data to third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok, through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

Deleting your data

You can disconnect Editor Lautaro at any time from your TikTok app settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions). Deleting the app from your computer removes all locally stored tokens and files.
