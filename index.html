Editor Lautaro

A personal desktop video editor that sends finished short videos to the creator's own TikTok account as drafts.

How it works

  1. The creator edits a short video on their own computer with Editor Lautaro.
  2. The creator connects their TikTok account once using "Login with TikTok".
  3. When a video is ready, the creator clicks "Send to TikTok". The video is uploaded to the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft.
  4. The creator opens the TikTok app, reviews the draft, adds sounds or edits if they want, and publishes it themselves.

Editor Lautaro never publishes anything automatically. Every post is reviewed and published by the creator inside the TikTok app.
